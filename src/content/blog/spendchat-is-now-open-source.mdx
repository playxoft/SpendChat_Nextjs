The repository is public: [github.com/playxoft/SpendChat_Nextjs](https://github.com/playxoft/SpendChat_Nextjs), under the **AGPL-3.0**.

## What's in it

The whole application, not a marketing subset — the auth checks, every query, the AI prompts, the CSV exporter, the migrations, the tests. If it runs on spendchat.app, it's in there.

To be exact about the arrangement, since it is the part you are right to check: this is **open core**. Everything in the repository is AGPL-3.0 and stays that way, nothing published here gets paywalled or withdrawn later, and Playxoft runs a commercial hosted offering on top of it — which is why contributions carry a CLA granting us a licence to use them there too. `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` say so in more detail, and the post below is where the distinction between this and source-available is worked through.

Also in the repository: `README.md` for running it yourself, `.env.example` documenting every setting, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `CLA.md`, and `SECURITY.md` for reporting a vulnerability privately.

## What changes for you

**Nothing, if you use [the hosted app](/blog/introducing-spendchat).** Same app, same URL, still free.

**The privacy claims became checkable.** "We don't sell your data" is a promise. "There is no bank integration in this repository" is something you can confirm yourself in about a minute.

**You can run your own copy.** Your database, your file storage, your keys — and your backups and upgrades too, which is the part worth being honest about.

**It stays open.** The AGPL is the licence that closes the hosted-service loophole in the plain GPL: anyone running a modified version as a service owes their users the source. That's deliberate, and it applies to us as much as to anyone.
