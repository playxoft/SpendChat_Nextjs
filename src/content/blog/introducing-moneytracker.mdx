Today we're sharing MoneyTracker: a minimal, fast, and private way to track your income and expenses. No connected bank accounts, no dashboards full of charts you never asked for — just a clean feed and a running balance.

## What it does

- Add transactions in a chat-style feed, or bulk import a whole list at once.
- Filter by date, type, and category, or search your notes.
- Export the current view to CSV, or print and save as PDF.
- Switch between light, dark, and system themes.

## Private and free

Your data is scoped to your account and shown only to you. There are no ads and your information is never sold. MoneyTracker is free to use — and open source, so you can see exactly how it works.

[Create an account](/sign-up) and send your first transaction; it takes less than a minute.
